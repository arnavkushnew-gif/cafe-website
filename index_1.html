About us

A neighbourhood coffee house

We started in 2019 with one espresso machine and a very patient landlord.

Everything is made in-house: we roast in small batches, bake each morning, and keep the menu short so every item is worth ordering. Mornings are for people who work here; evenings are for people who linger. Both are welcome.
